公司介绍 / About Us

移康智能创立于2011年，是专注于人工智能门控系统的技术提供商和系统服务商，主要产品有智能猫眼、可视门铃、可视智能锁方案、AI智能门系统及可视对讲门禁系统等。公司致力于将无线通信技术、物联传感技术及人工智能技术与日常生活相结合，助力客户打造更安全、更便利、更舒适、更智能的AI+IOT场景生态，让生活更安心、让社会更安全！

Founded in 2011, Eques is a technology provider and system service provider focused on artificial-intelligence access-control systems. Its main products include smart peepholes, video doorbells, video smart-lock solutions, AI smart-door systems, and video-intercom access-control systems. The company brings wireless communication, IoT sensing, and artificial intelligence into everyday life, helping customers build a safer, more convenient, more comfortable, and more intelligent AI+IoT ecosystem.

目前公司在智能猫眼可视门铃、物联网可视智能锁方案、智能门系统方案上紧跟国内领先、国际先进水平，不断研发创新。目前累计申请发明专利约200项、PCT国际专利3项，并获得国家级荣誉近10项，包括获得2019年世界人工智能大会产品创新成果奖、第11届、第13届精瑞科学技术奖人居智能化创新奖（隶属于科技部，国家人居与城市建设最高奖励）、第三届中国创新创业大赛全国总决赛优秀企业奖（科技部）、首届中国（上海）国际发明创新展览会金奖、第29届上海市优秀发明选拔赛优秀发明金奖等等。

In smart video doorbells, IoT video smart-lock solutions, and smart-door systems, the company keeps pace with leading standards in China and advanced standards worldwide, and continues to innovate. It has filed about 200 invention patent applications and 3 PCT international patents, and has received nearly 10 national honors. These include the Product Innovation Award at the 2019 World Artificial Intelligence Conference; the Habitat Intelligence Innovation Award at the 11th and 13th Jingrui Science and Technology Awards (under the Ministry of Science and Technology, among the highest honors in habitat and urban development in China); the Outstanding Enterprise Award at the national final of the 3rd China Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition (Ministry of Science and Technology); the Gold Award at the first China (Shanghai) International Invention and Innovation Exhibition; and the Gold Award at the 29th Shanghai Outstanding Invention Selection.

公司获得美国高通（Qualcomm）、原子创投（ATOM Ventures）等战略投资。目前公司在深圳、中山及Palo Alto（硅谷）设立分支机构，产品远销全球90多个国家和地区。

The company has received strategic investment from Qualcomm and ATOM Ventures. It has offices in Shenzhen, Zhongshan, and Palo Alto (Silicon Valley), and its products are sold in more than 90 countries and regions.

公司和国内外众多知名品牌有深度合作，包括ASSA ABLOY、Yale、Panasonic、海尔、海信、美的、万科、中海地产、世茂地产等，也是华为、阿里、腾讯等的生态战略合作伙伴。

The company works closely with well-known brands in China and abroad, including ASSA ABLOY, Yale, Panasonic, Haier, Hisense, Midea, Vanke, China Overseas Property, and Shimao. It is also an ecosystem strategic partner of Huawei, Alibaba, and Tencent.

EQUES源自希腊神话众神之王宙斯的盾“Aegis（埃奎斯）”，由宙斯和火神共同打造，是宙斯的护身法宝。EQUES品牌初心是“心安即为家”，让智能、安全、便捷的门控系统守护千家万户，打造家庭的守护神。移康智能/EQUES正努力成为全球领先的智能门控系统方案行业领军品牌。

EQUES takes its name from Aegis, the shield of Zeus, king of the gods in Greek mythology, forged by Zeus and the god of fire as his protective emblem. The brand’s founding idea is “a home is where the heart is at ease”: intelligent, secure, and convenient access-control systems that protect households. Eques is working to become a leading global brand in smart access-control system solutions.
